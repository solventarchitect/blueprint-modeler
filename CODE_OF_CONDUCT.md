# Code of Conduct

Blueprint Modeler is a small open-source project. We want taking part in it to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with questions; everyone starts somewhere.
- Credit other people's work, and cite sources for rules (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact form at [mikereams.com/contact](https://mikereams.com/contact). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who don't follow this code.

This code applies in the repository and in project spaces elsewhere when someone represents the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
